How pricing works

Labor is fixed

The price you book at is the price you pay for the work, even if the job takes longer.

Parts are approved by you

Parts are quoted on site and sent to your phone. Nothing is installed until you approve.

Inspections are deducted

Where a fault needs diagnosis first, the inspection price comes off the repair if you go ahead.

Are you a technician?

Get job requests in your trade and area, at prices you see upfront. You keep 80% of the labor and 80% of the margin on parts and products from our catalog.
